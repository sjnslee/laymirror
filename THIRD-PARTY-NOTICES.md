`plugin.js`.

## docx-preview

Copyright Volodymyr Baydalka. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
<https://github.com/VolodymyrBaydalka/docxjs>

Renders the school's `.docx` for page view and resolves its styles —
`basedOn` chains, theme fonts and theme colours — so laymirror does not
have to reimplement word's style cascade.
